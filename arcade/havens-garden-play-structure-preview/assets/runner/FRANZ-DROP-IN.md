# Runner art — Franz v1 wired

Runtime uses Franz HAVENS-GARDEN-RUNNER-V1 finals under `assets/runner/`.
Placeholders remain in `placeholders/` as load fallback only.

## In use
- Haven: haven-run-01…06, haven-jump/duck/lean-left/lean-right/stumble/pick (256×320 anchors)
- Obstacles: obs-hay/puddle/fence/rock/wheelbarrow/log/sprinkler + obs-sprinkler-water
- Collectibles: flower-daisy/tulip/sunflower/bluebell
- Skies + horizons: sky-{day,sunset,dusk}, horizon-{day,sunset,dusk} (crossfade)
- World textures: tex-dirt, tex-grass (256×256 projected), tex-hedgerow (512×96 separators)

## NOT used in-game
Phone reference plates (`phone_composite_*`, `phone_field_no_haven_*`) are LOOK ONLY —
do not scroll or draw them as the world.
